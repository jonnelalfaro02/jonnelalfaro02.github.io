Complaint filing & escalations

When a dispute round stalls, I escalate it

If a bureau, furnisher, or collector verifies an item without a real investigation, ignores a letter, or misses the 30-day deadline, I prepare and file the complaint with a clear timeline, the dispute history, and supporting documents attached.

Federal

CFPB complaints

File complaints on the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau portal against Equifax, Experian, TransUnion, creditors, and collectors, then track the company's response and follow up.

State

State Attorney General

Submit consumer complaints to the client's State Attorney General office for FCRA and FDCPA violations, adding extra pressure on repeat offenders.

Reputation

Better Business Bureau

File BBB complaints against collection agencies and creditors that won't respond, and document every reply in the client's file.
